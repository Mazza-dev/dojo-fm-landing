PRIVACY & FAMILY DATA

Privacy Policy

How Dojo FM handles parent accounts, child profiles, listening progress, and story challenge responses.

FAMILY INFORMATIONPrivacy PolicyTerms of ServiceChild SafetyDelete Account

1. Who this policy is for

Dojo FM is a family audio-learning service. An adult parent or legal guardian creates and controls the family account. Children use profiles created and managed by that adult.

This policy explains the information processed when a family uses the Dojo FM mobile app, web experience, and related services.

2. Information we process

  • Parent account information: email address, authentication records, and account settings.
  • Child profile information supplied by a parent: profile name, age, grade, interests, learning goal, preferred language, and learning preferences.
  • Learning activity: stories and episodes opened, listening position, time listened, completion status, saved stories, achievements, and rewards.
  • Story challenge activity: the child’s typed response, hints used, AI feedback, verdict, and correction.
  • Preferences stored on the device or in the family account, including playback speed, sound-effect preference, sleep timer, listening goals, and reminder time.
  • Limited technical records processed by our service providers, such as IP address, device or browser information, request timestamps, and security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

3. Information we do not ask children for

Dojo FM does not ask children for a personal email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or social-media account. The current app does not contain third-party advertising, public profiles, direct messages, or public posting.

4. How we use information

  • Create and secure the parent-controlled family account.
  • Personalize age-appropriate story recommendations and learning prompts.
  • Remember playback position and show progress, achievements, and weekly summaries.
  • Generate feedback for story challenges and preserve the family’s challenge history.
  • Provide reminders requested by the parent, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

5. AI story challenges

When a child submits a story challenge, the challenge claim, accepted science explanation, reading-level preference, and typed response are sent through a protected Supabase Edge Function to Fal.ai and the selected language-model provider so that feedback can be generated.

The request is designed not to include the parent’s email address or the child’s profile name. Families should not type names, contact details, school information, or other identifying information into a challenge response. AI feedback may be incomplete or incorrect and should be treated as educational guidance, not professional advice.

6. Service providers

We use service providers only to operate Dojo FM. Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and server functions. Fal.ai and its model infrastructure process story-challenge prompts and responses. Apple or Google may process information when the app is installed, updated, or when device-level features are used under their own terms.

We do not sell personal information or use child information for targeted advertising. We do not allow service providers to use child information for their own advertising.

7. Parent choices and control

  • Parents can review, update, switch, or delete child profiles from the protected grown-ups area.
  • Parents can change listening and reminder preferences at any time.
  • Parents can delete the complete family account and associated child data from the app.
  • Parents may contact support@dojofm.app to ask for access, correction, export, or deletion assistance.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep account and learning information while the family account is active and only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Deleting a child profile removes its associated progress, settings, challenge history, rewards, and achievements. Deleting the family account removes the parent account and associated child data, subject to limited records we may be legally required to retain.

9. Security and international processing

We use access controls, encrypted connections, private audio storage, and row-level database security designed to keep one family’s data separate from another family’s data. No system is completely secure, so parents should use a unique password and keep their parent PIN private.

Our providers may process information in countries other than the family’s country. We require information to be handled for the purposes described in this policy and subject to applicable protections.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Dojo FM changes. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will be presented to parents when appropriate. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@dojofm.app.
