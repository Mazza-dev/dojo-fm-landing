ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your account

Parents can delete the complete family account and the children’s associated learning data from the app.

FAMILY INFORMATIONPrivacy PolicyTerms of ServiceChild SafetyDelete Account

Delete in the app

Sign in to Dojo FM as the parent account holder. Open the Grown-ups area and unlock it with the parent PIN. Choose the account deletion option, read the warning, type DELETE, and confirm. The app will sign you out after the deletion request succeeds.

What is deleted

  • The parent login and family account.
  • Every child profile and preference.
  • Listening progress, saved stories, and sessions.
  • Achievements, rewards, and challenge responses.

Some limited records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and dispute-resolution purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or need help with a deletion request, email support@dojofm.app from the address connected to the parent account. Please do not include sensitive details about a child in your message.

Store subscriptions

If paid access is introduced, deleting a Dojo FM account will not automatically cancel an Apple or Google subscription. The parent will need to cancel it in the relevant store account.
