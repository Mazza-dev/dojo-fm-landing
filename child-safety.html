Child Safety

The controls and design choices that keep Dojo FM’s listening experience family-managed.

FAMILY INFORMATIONPrivacy PolicyTerms of ServiceChild SafetyDelete Account

A parent-controlled experience

An adult creates the family account and child profiles. Progress controls, profile management, legal information, and account deletion are kept in the PIN-protected grown-ups area.

No social pressure or advertising

  • No public child profiles, follower counts, direct messages, or public posting.
  • No third-party behavioural advertising or ads targeted using a child’s activity.
  • No access to contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone in the current app.
  • No external web links in the child listening experience.

Safer AI challenges

Story challenges are limited to short educational responses. The app tells families not to enter identifying or sensitive information. Challenge prompts pass through our server function, length limits, and a fixed educational instruction before reaching the AI provider.

AI output can still be wrong. Parents should review any answer that seems confusing, unsafe, or inappropriate and report it to us.

Parent controls

  • Review weekly listening, completed stories, challenges, and achievements.
  • Adjust playback, sound effects, sleep timer, listening goals, and reminders.
  • Edit or delete a child profile and its associated learning history.
  • Delete the entire family account and associated data.

Report a concern

For a story, AI response, privacy, or child-safety concern, contact support@dojofm.app. Include the story title and episode number, but do not send sensitive information about a child.
